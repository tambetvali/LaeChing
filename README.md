
Logarithmic value is drawn here, relatively growing or shrinking. For example, linear growth from x = y = 0 through y = -0.5 or y = 0.5 / sqrt(2) throguh x = 1.
- 1 year could be time; 1 person could be space; 1 step could be depth.
- Octave 1
- Differential 1
- Complexity: 1
- Quantum-hilbert's complexity.

## 2 lines of I Ching is second rank: first dimension.

Linear value is drawn here, relatively growing or shrinking. For example, linear growth from x = y = 0 through y = -1 or y = 1 throguh x = 1.
- 2 years could be time; 2 persons could be space; 2 steps could be depth.
- Octave 2
- Linear: differential 0 / exponential 0
- Complexity: 2
- Newtonian-eucleidean complexity.

## 3 lines of I Ching is first rank: first dimension.

Exponential value is drawn here, relatively growing or shrinking. For example, linear growth from x = y = 0 through y = -2 or y = 2 throguh x = 1.
- Octave 3
- Exponential 1
- Complexity: 4
- Einsteinean-maxwellian complexity.

## 6 lines

Hilbert's internal and external space are both 3 lines:
- Both form internal or external infinity of precision and depth.
- Both can carry different octave systems, because in the center they could meet infinitely to higher precision.

# The 64-State Lattice

The complete hexagram space forms:

```text
2^6 = 64
```

states.

Each state is connected to six immediate neighbours.

Changing one line:

```text
1 change
```

Changing two lines:

```text
2 simultaneous changes
```

and so on until:

```text
6 changes
```

which produces the inverse hexagram.
